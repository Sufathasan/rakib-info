I’m Kamrul Hassan Rakib, a graphic designer and branding specialist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My portfolio brings together work across brand identity, logo design, packaging, product labels, and visual communication. I use Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Figma, and Canva to shape ideas into clear, considered visual expressions.
